# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We aim to make participation in the Memora project a respectful and welcoming
experience for everyone, regardless of background or identity.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Being respectful and considerate.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its community.
- Respecting differing viewpoints and experiences.

## Unacceptable behavior

Examples include harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language,
deliberate intimidation, and publishing another person's private information
without permission.

## Maintainer responsibilities

Project maintainers may edit, reject, or remove contributions that violate
this code and may restrict participation when necessary.

## Reporting concerns

Contact the repository owner privately through their GitHub profile to report
conduct concerns. Please avoid sharing private or sensitive information
publicly.

This code of conduct applies to project spaces and activities associated with
Memora.
